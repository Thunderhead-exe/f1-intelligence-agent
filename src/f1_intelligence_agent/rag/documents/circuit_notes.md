# Circuit Notes

Monza: A high-speed circuit where straight-line performance, braking stability, and low-drag efficiency are often important. Avoid assuming cause from speed alone.

Monaco: A low-speed street circuit where track position, precision, and traffic can strongly affect lap time. Overtaking is difficult.

Silverstone: A circuit with many high-speed corners. Sector patterns can reflect aerodynamic balance, tyre state, traffic, or wind, but need validation.

Spa: A long lap with elevation change and mixed corner types. Sector comparisons should account for the wide variety of corner and straight profiles.

Barcelona: A balanced circuit with a mix of corner speeds. It is often used to evaluate broad car performance patterns.

Bahrain: Tyre degradation and traction zones can be important. Degradation should be described as a possible pattern, not proof.

Singapore: A street circuit with heat, traction zones, and high precision demands. Session interruptions and traffic can matter.
